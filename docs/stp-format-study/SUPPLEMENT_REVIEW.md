## Resolved review findings

The original `tc-budgetshift-p085` rule could fail on legitimate attention states: for N=2 and all 32 heads selecting one key, B=32 but no head meets the recipient condition. At the parent's instruction, the proposal now defines an atomic whole-row TC no-op whenever eligible capacity C<B. It records B, C, actual added keys, unspent budget and infeasible-row frequency. It does not partially allocate or invent replacement recipients. B=0 is counted separately. This makes the proposed algorithm total; it weakens the matched-budget interpretation on infeasible rows and the revised text states that limitation. Actual frequency remains unmeasured.

The count-matched oracle explicitly takes floor4's count. Both selector controls label their inherited TC logit statistics, and the co-primary aggregation explicitly averages per-head centered RMSE over the 192 INT-source rows at each p. These changes add no cells or experiments.

## Checked and acceptable scope

- The fixed midpoint algebra is correct: M=16H+7.5, R=x-M, and the eight A/B/C subsets recover the exact full dot. Doubled-code ranges and both displayed dot bounds are correct; the expanded midpoint dot has coefficients 256, 120, 120 and 56.25D. The asymmetric endpoint bin and zero mapping are disclosed rather than empirically corrected.
- Exact integer accumulation before the specified ordered FP32 scale operations avoids separately rounded term addition. Inherited-control mask checks and saved boundary discrepancies are necessary because mathematical equality alone does not guarantee execution identity. The proposal retains this requirement.
- Inventory is consistent: 54 traces ×32 heads=1728 source/head rows; 12 logit definitions plus two selector definitions give 24192 metric rows. Five model cells imply five primary smoke and five primary full processes, with 30 requested capture records. Cache/replay invocations and any allowed retry must remain separately enumerated rather than being hidden by the primary-process count.
- Fixed inputs identify local estimator/selector effects. Independent trajectories and layer0/16 interventions cannot uniquely allocate total NLL loss or isolate nonlinear interactions. Phase B acknowledges these limits. The budget control matches extra counts only on identical inputs; independent runs do not provide exact matching of counts, final MACs or latency.
- Phase A keeps all heads, legal-key semantics, original full final operands, concentration strata defined by the reference, and tail failures. Correlation and the narrow six-capture smoke scope preclude a corpus-rate or significance claim. Floor4 changes the selector rule beyond nominal Top-p; it does not preserve a matched probability threshold or prove a pure ranking-only effect.
- There is no proposed fitting on evaluation data. The p=.85 stress point is already informed by prior test results and is appropriately disclosed; no unbiased generalization or tuned-optimum claim follows. Calibration/fitted gates remain outside scope.
- Original-source execution, GQA, target coverage, CE/NLL precision, trace-off full runs, no quality-based reruns, retained negative results and separate phase approvals are preserved. Logical four-bit storage is distinguished from actual wider buffers, full-source retention, correction work and unmeasured hardware performance.
